Suite Field

This is the field phone. It is separate from the desk. It uses the same company.

The button saves the file. It does not open by itself. That is normal.

  1. Tap the button. Wait until it says the download finished.
  2. Tap the finished download. On a phone that is the bar at the top, or the Downloads app.
  3. Tap Install.
  4. Open Suite Field. Sign in. If the desk gave you a field link, open that link in this phone.

If the phone asks, allow installs from the browser.
